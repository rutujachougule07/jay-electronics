# Jay Electronics corporate website

## Goal
Build a responsive, multi-page corporate website that preserves Jay Electronics’ existing brand identity while replacing the dated presentation with a precise, modern engineering-led experience.

## Pages and navigation
- **Home:** Header → full-width two-image slider → verified statistics → About → What We Deliver → Brand Logos → Industries → Our Team → Footer.
- **About:** Company profile, story since 1989, experience, vision and mission, and reasons to choose Jay Electronics.
- **Services:** Dedicated capability groups for CCTV, networking, fibre, EPABX/IP-PBX, audio visual, fire security, telecom infrastructure, office automation, and solar solutions.
- **Projects:** Project-category presentation for city surveillance, government, healthcare, courts, networking, EPABX, and AV—without inventing client names or unsupported project counts.
- **Contact:** Verified address, phone, email, location link, and a polished enquiry form with clear client-side confirmation.

## Visual direction
- Preserve the existing navy, red, white, cool-grey, and blue identity, using semantic design tokens throughout.
- Use Plus Jakarta Sans with strong editorial hierarchy, restrained borders, compact spacing, and modest corner radii.
- Favor full-width image sections, structured data bands, crisp service grids, and subtle motion over glass effects or decorative gradients.
- Build a responsive desktop navigation and accessible mobile menu.

## Content and imagery
- Use verified facts from the official website: founded in 1989, electronics and telecom background, thousands of installations, supported services, partner brands, sectors, and Sangli contact details.
- Download and preserve the official logo plus suitable inauguration/event and CCTV imagery from the company’s own website, storing them through the project asset flow.
- Use recognizable brand wordmarks only for brands verified on the official site; label the section neutrally rather than overstating partnership status.
- Keep the team section organization-led because individual team details are not reliably published.

## Implementation details
- Create reusable site shell, navigation, footer, slider, section heading, data-card, and content-card components.
- Keep service, project, partner, and industry content in shared typed data arrays.
- Use TanStack Router’s native page routes and metadata for every page.
- Use the existing button and carousel foundations where appropriate, plus accessible controls and reduced-motion support.
- Validate desktop and mobile layouts, menu and slider behavior, links, form state, console output, and page metadata.

## Source limitations
No PDFs or mobile screenshot were available in the uploaded files. The build will follow the requested section order and rely only on facts verifiable from the official website; unsupported statistics and team identities will be omitted.
